- (constraint) Rating is outcome-based 0-3 defined by what the hitter could do (3 full swing, 2 attack with adjustment, 1 free ball/tip only, 0 unhittable or setting fault); never 'how it felt'.

- (constraint) Views emphasise multi-week trends over single sessions (sessions are noisy).
- (constraint) Access control is part of the v1 design, not retrofitted.
- (constraint) v1 data scope: one setter, one team.
