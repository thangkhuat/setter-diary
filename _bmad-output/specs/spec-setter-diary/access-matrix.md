# Access Matrix

All access is scoped to the user's own team; no one outside a team sees its data. A team may have several setters; a player may hold both roles.

| User | Can log | Can view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setter | Their own sets, with full detail; their own sessions; team roster; set-type list; grant or revoke team-wide view | Their own diary only: progress across their hitters and set types, including each hitter's overall rating of their sets. Never another setter's diary |
| Hitter (teammate) | Overall 0–3 rating of the sets they received from a setter | Own progress (v1: quality of sets they received); each team setter's consistency trend; own row of each setter's hitter × set-type grid. Never other hitters' data |
| Member with team-wide view (setter or hitter) | As their role | All of their team's stats: every setter's diary and every hitter's data |
| Any user | Delete own account (data anonymised, kept); export own data as CSV | — |
| Setter, AI discussion | Start and continue own AI conversations | Own AI conversations only. No one else, including team-wide view holders, can see them |

A player removed from a team loses access to its data; their history stays with the team.
